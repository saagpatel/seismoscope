# Privacy Policy: Seismoscope

Seismoscope processes accelerometer data solely on your device. Raw motion samples and detected-event history are not uploaded.

To compare a detected vibration with the earthquake catalog, the app sends region coordinates and an event-time search window to the public U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) earthquake service. Until you choose a city preset or enter custom coordinates, requests use the San Francisco preset. Custom coordinates can represent a precise location. Requests also include the response format, search radius, minimum magnitude, sort order, and result limit. USGS also receives ordinary network information such as your device's IP address. Seismoscope does not use Core Location or obtain your device's GPS location.

If you choose View on USGS in a matched event's detail sheet, the app opens that event's USGS web page. USGS handles catalog requests and page visits under its own privacy practices. We do not control how long USGS keeps request logs.

All signal processing and event storage happen locally on your iPhone or iPad.

No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
